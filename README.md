## Overview

Large language models are increasingly used to summarize research papers, draft literature reviews, and suggest references. However, citations generated by these systems can be unreliable. A citation may be fabricated, may contain incorrect authors or publication details, may refer to an unrelated source, or may change when the same claim is requested again in a later conversation.

Citation drift refers to the instability of citations across repeated, rephrased, or multi-turn interactions with an AI system. It includes citation mutation, citation loss, citation fabrication, and changes caused by retrieval systems. This topic is important because inaccurate references can mislead researchers, weaken literature reviews, and reduce trust in AI-assisted scholarship.

This repository organizes verified resources on citation accuracy, hallucination detection, factual consistency, retrieval-augmented generation, and methods for verifying AI-generated references.

## Citation Integrity Audit

A systematic audit was conducted on 10 references selected from the AI-generated paper.

- 6 references were fully verified.
- 4 references contained incorrect or incomplete metadata.
- No fabricated references were found in the selected audit sample.
- Authenticity score: **90/100**.
