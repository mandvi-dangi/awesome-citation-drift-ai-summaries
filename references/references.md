# Verified Research Papers

This collection contains verified scholarly papers on citation reliability, citation drift, hallucination detection, factual consistency, and retrieval-grounded verification.

## Citation Accuracy and Citation Fabrication

- **Fabrication and Errors in the Bibliographic Citations Generated by ChatGPT**  
  William H. Walters and Emily I. Wilder, 2023, *Scientific Reports*.  
  [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-023-41032-5)  
  Examines fabricated and inaccurate citations generated by ChatGPT.

- **High Rates of Fabricated and Inaccurate References in ChatGPT-Generated Medical Content**  
  M. Bhattacharyya, V. M. Miller, D. Bhattacharyya, and L. E. Miller, 2023, *Cureus*.  
  [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.7759/cureus.39238)  
  Audits the reliability of references generated for medical content.

- **Evaluation of Large Language Model Performance and Reliability for Citations and References in Scholarly Writing: Cross-Disciplinary Study**  
  J. Mugaanyi, L. Cai, S. Cheng, C. Lu, and J. Huang, 2024, *Journal of Medical Internet Research*.  
  [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.2196/52935)  
  Evaluates citation and reference reliability across scholarly disciplines.

## Citation Drift and Hallucination Surveys

- **Citation Drift: Measuring Reference Stability in Multi-Turn LLM Conversations**  
  G. S. S. Ram, 2025, *Proceedings of the Third Workshop for Artificial Intelligence for Scientific Publications*.  
  [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2025.wasp-main.20/)  
  Introduces citation drift as reference mutation, loss, or fabrication across extended LLM conversations.

- **Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation**  
  Ziwei Ji et al., 2023, *ACM Computing Surveys*.  
  [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3571730)  
  Provides a foundational taxonomy of hallucination in generated text.

- **A Survey on Hallucination in Large Language Models: Principles, Taxonomy, Challenges, and Open Questions**  
  Lei Huang et al., 2025, *ACM Transactions on Information Systems*.  
  [Paper / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1145/3703155)  
  Reviews hallucination mechanisms, detection approaches, and open challenges in LLMs.

## Detection and Verification Methods

- **Evaluating the Factual Consistency of Abstractive Text Summarization**  
  Wojciech Kryściński, Bryan McCann, Caiming Xiong, and Richard Socher, 2020, *EMNLP*.  
  [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2020.emnlp-main.750/)  
  Introduces FactCC, an early method for factual-consistency evaluation in summaries.

- **SelfCheckGPT: Zero-Resource Black-Box Hallucination Detection for Generative Large Language Models**  
  Potsawee Manakul, Adian Liusie, and Mark J. F. Gales, 2023, *EMNLP*.  
  [Paper](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.557/)  
  Detects hallucinations through consistency across multiple sampled responses.

- **Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks**  
  Patrick Lewis et al., 2020, *NeurIPS*.  
  [Paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2020/hash/6b493230205f780e1bc26945df7481e5-Abstract.html)  
  Introduces retrieval-augmented generation, a major approach for grounding model outputs in retrieved evidence.

- **CiteCheck: Retrieval-Grounded Detection of LLM Citation Hallucinations in Scientific Text**  
  Khashayar Khajavi, Shaghayegh Sadeghi, Rise Adhikari, and Alexander Tessier, 2026, *arXiv*.  
  [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27700)  
  Proposes retrieval-grounded verification for detecting inaccurate and fabricated citations.
