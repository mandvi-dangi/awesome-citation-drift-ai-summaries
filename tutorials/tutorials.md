# Tutorials and Learning Resources

## ACL Anthology

**Purpose:** A major open-access digital library for computational-linguistics research papers.  
**Use in this topic:** Find verified papers on hallucination detection, factual consistency, summarization, and citation drift.  
[Visit ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/)

## Crossref Metadata Search

**Purpose:** Searches DOI records and publication metadata.  
**Use in this topic:** Verify whether AI-generated citations exist and whether their bibliographic details are correct.  
[Visit Crossref Search](https://search.crossref.org/)

## Semantic Scholar

**Purpose:** A scholarly search engine for discovering research papers and citation relationships.  
**Use in this topic:** Find and verify academic sources related to AI-generated citations and hallucinations.  
[Visit Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/)

## Hugging Face Course

**Purpose:** Free learning material for transformer models and natural-language processing.  
**Use in this topic:** Learn how transformer-based models can be used for retrieval, classification, and hallucination detection.  
[Visit Hugging Face Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course)

## Stanford CS224N: Natural Language Processing with Deep Learning

**Purpose:** University course materials covering modern NLP, transformers, language models, and retrieval.  
**Use in this topic:** Builds the technical foundation needed to understand AI-generated summaries and factual-consistency methods.  
[Visit CS224N](https://web.stanford.edu/class/cs224n/)
